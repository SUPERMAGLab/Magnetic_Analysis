Background Subtraction

Choose left and right outer-field windows. The tool fits separate straight lines to the negative- and positive-field regions, averages their slopes, and subtracts only the linear field-dependent background using moment − field × slope.

Tip: the defaults are seeded from the outer 15% of the field range. Refine the limits numerically so the left window captures the negative-field linear region and the right window captures the positive-field linear region.
